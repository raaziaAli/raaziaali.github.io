How a module works

Every topic moves from intuition to real data in the same order.

1

Explore a simulation

Change one parameter at a time and see its effect, like the wedge model above.

2

Work through a notebook

Reproduce the result step by step in code, with the reasoning written alongside.

3

Apply it to real data

Use public datasets — well logs, seismic surveys, field measurements — to answer a real question.

4

Check with an AI tutor

An assistant that gives hints instead of answers and adapts to the learner's level.

About

Classroom of the Future was founded by Raazia Ali, a geophysicist and mathematics teacher. It began as a set of interactive tools for her own middle and high school classes and is growing into a single place to learn earth science and mathematics at every level.
