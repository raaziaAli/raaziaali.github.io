Module 5

Find the sand on a gamma-ray log

Shale is naturally more radioactive than clean sand, so a gamma-ray log reads high in shale and low in sand. Geologists pick a cutoff value: below it is sand, above it is shale.

  1. Drag the cutoff and watch the sand (yellow) appear on the log.
  2. Compare your answer with the core taken from the well. Can you score above 95%?
